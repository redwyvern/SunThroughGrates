## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added

- Sunlight passes through iron cage floors, iron cage walls and iron grates,
  so crops grow beneath them.
- `Ignored prefabs` config setting listing the pieces that let sunlight
  through. Changes apply live.
